# Enterprise Reasoning

An open initiative developing a vendor-neutral standard for representing reasoning across people, software, and AI.

## Start here

- **[Read the white paper](https://github.com/Enterprise-Reasoning/standard/blob/main/Enterprise-Reasoning-White-Paper-2026-09.pdf)** — the September 2026 starting reference.
- **[Learn how to participate](https://github.com/Enterprise-Reasoning/standard/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)** — ask questions, share use cases, review proposals, or submit changes.
- **[Understand the decision process](https://github.com/Enterprise-Reasoning/standard/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md)** — public review, objections, decision records, and reconsideration.
- **[Explore the roadmap](https://github.com/Enterprise-Reasoning/standard/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)** — the path from white paper to tested interoperability.

## Everyone can participate

You do not need organization membership to contribute to the public discussion. Business experts, implementers, researchers, standards practitioners, and technical writers are welcome.

[Open a question or proposal](https://github.com/Enterprise-Reasoning/standard/issues/new) or [review existing issues](https://github.com/Enterprise-Reasoning/standard/issues). Start with the contribution guide for the proposal format and review expectations.

**Current stage:** community formation and specification development. The white paper is a starting point; no normative specification or conformance certification has been adopted. The initial governance process is published for transparent participation and further community refinement.
